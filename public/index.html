The raid loop

Every round is a risky heist.

Start with a few resources and a vulnerable core. By the end of the round, your fort should be strong enough to survive a breach—and your route to the enemy should be ready.

1

Prepare your fort

Move with WASD, gather nearby resource nodes with E, and use right-click to place defensive structures.

2

Raid a rival

During the raid phase, attack players and structures. Use the number keys to switch between build pieces.

3

Extract the prize

Touch an enemy core, carry it home, and reach your own core before another player knocks you out.
